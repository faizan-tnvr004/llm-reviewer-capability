# Hand-off: code extension results

Every number below traces to `run_code_data/RESULTS.md` (generated by `analyze_code.py` from `run_code_data/log.jsonl`) or to `run_code_data/E3_AUDIT.md`.

## Headline

On 125 LiveCodeBench problems, the executor (`gemini-3.1-flash-lite`) solved 57 unaided (45.6%). No reviewer produced a significant accuracy gain after Holm correction:
- self-review reached 60 (+2.4 points);
- the cross-family reviewer `openai/gpt-oss-20b` reached 63 (+4.8 points, 2 broken / 8 fixed, Holm p = 0.44);
- the weak Llama 3.1 8B reviewer stayed at 57;
- the public-sample-test reviewer reached 63 (+4.8 points, 0 broken / 6 fixed, raw p = 0.031, Holm p = 0.16).

Self-review falsely rejected 20 of 57 correct programs (35.1%), the same rate as in the math study. But unlike math, where the executor ignored 15 of 18 false rejections, here it rewrote 19 of 20 correct programs, and 4 of those rewrites broke a working program. The public sample tests never rejected a correct program (0 of 57), but 19 of 68 wrong programs (27.9%) passed every sample test. A second Docker pass changed the fingerprint of 3 of 328 distinct programs: two borderline timeouts and one artifact of the laptop sleeping. Neither the confirmatory results nor any condition comparison depends on them.

## Hypotheses

- **H1, supported**: C1 false-rejection rate 20/57 = 35.1% (Wilson 95% CI 24–48%), at or above the pre-registered 20%.
- **H2, not supported**: 1 of 20 C1 false rejections left the program unchanged by AST hash (0 of 20 by text hash). The pre-registered threshold was more than 50%.
- **H3, not supported**: C2 50.4% vs C0 45.6%, exact McNemar b = 2, c = 8, raw p = 0.109, Holm p = 0.438.
- **H4, supported**: C4 false-rejection rate 0/57 = 0.0% (at most 5%), and false accepts 19/68 (more than 0).
- **E2 (exploratory)**:
  - A0 accuracy: 34/78 before the 2025-01-31 cutoff vs 23/47 after (Fisher p = 0.58).
  - H1 and H4 still hold on the 47 post-cutoff problems.
  - H2 is inconclusive there (7 false rejections, fewer than 10).
  - H3 is not supported there.
- **E1 (exploratory, agent-labelled)**: a blind LLM labeller rated 147 of 154 C1–C3 critiques `specific` and 7 `vague`. The vague ones are mostly from self-review: C1 6/75, C2 0/76, C3 1/3. The executor revised its code after 146 of 147 specific critiques and 7 of 7 vague ones (AST hash), so adoption is near-total and specificity barely varies. The 30-item human agreement check is not done yet. Report kappa with E1, or state that E1 is agent-labelled with no human check (Amendment 1, Section 6). Full output: `run_code_data/e1/E1_RESULTS_agent.txt`.
- **E3 (exploratory)**: 3 of 328 programs (21 of 1,250 records) disagreed between passes, all PASS to TIMEOUT.

## Three surprising observations

1. **The executor does not ignore false critiques in code; it rewrites.** This reverses the math finding behind H2. In the C1 rows of `log.jsonl` where `a0_correct = true` and `verdict = REJECT` (20 rows), 19 have `final_ast_hash != a0_ast_hash`. The only unchanged one is `abc377_d`. The rewrites still broke 4 correct programs: `abc385_e`, `abc379_f`, `abc400_d` and `arc193_a`, the rows with `final_correct = false`.
2. **Sample tests are a safe but leaky reviewer.** C4 broke nothing and fixed 6 programs: `abc389_f`, `abc388_g`, `abc374_e`, `abc384_e`, `abc376_e` and `abc386_d`, the C4 rows with `a0_correct = false` and `final_correct = true`. That's the most favourable discordant split of any condition (0 broken / 6 fixed). But 19 of the 68 wrong A0 programs pass every public sample (the C4 rows with `a0_correct = false` and `a0_public_pass = true`; for example `arc192_a`, `abc375_f` and `abc384_f`), so C4 cannot catch them.
3. **The weak reviewer almost never objects.** Llama 3.1 8B rejected 3 of 125 programs (`abc394_f`, `abc390_e`, `arc188_a`, all wrong before and after revision) and accepted 65 of 68 wrong programs: recall 4.4%. That matches the math study, where the weak reviewer changed none of the 100 final answers.

## Caveats to carry into emails and the paper

- With N = 125, effects of a few points cannot be ruled out. Report the intervals and never write "no difference".
- C3 ran the same Llama weights locally (Ollama, Q8_0) because Groq retired the original model, so it is not identical to the math study's C3.
- 12 of 125 C2 reviews (9.6%) were empty or malformed and scored as ACCEPT, as pre-registered. The math run had 16 of 100.
- Exact token counts are missing for 28 problems. Token cost is reported on the 97 problems with exact counts, with estimates shown separately.
- Run incidents (provider requests that never returned, Gemini outages, an Ollama auto-update reverted before use) changed no outcome. All are listed with times in `ANALYSIS_PLAN_CODE_AMENDMENT.md`.
